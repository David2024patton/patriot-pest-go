# ENDGOAL: Patriot Pest Control

The end state this project exists to reach, in plain words. TODO.md and PLAN.md are how we get here; this file is how we know we are done. Work continues until this end goal is reached. When David restates or extends the end goal, this file is updated the same day and PLAN/TODO follow.

## The end state

Patriot Pest Control runs as a complete operation on the AlphaFlux platform, with David handling marketing and tech for Skyler Rose's company, and this website as its public front door. A homeowner in Eastern Washington or North Idaho finds Patriot Pest Control, sees the full company name and the veteran-owned line everywhere words appear, calls the one public number or sends a form, and that lead lands in the CRM with its source attached and an alert proven delivered, every time. The phone system, scheduling, win-back outreach to inactive customers, social ads, and daily page posting all run as one system around the site, and spend decisions come from measured channel contribution instead of platform claims.

## How we will know it is reached

- The live site serves only Eastern Washington and North Idaho content, with the full name Patriot Pest Control in every company reference and (509) 471-5767 as the only public number, proven by a rendered crawl with zero violations.
- Every website form lead creates a CRM record with source attribution and phone required, and the alert leg is proven delivered by carrier status or recipient confirmation, not acceptance.
- A scheduled synthetic lead test passes end to end (form, CRM, attribution, alert) without a real customer, and its test contact is cleaned up.
- The site is probed continuously and an outage alerts the operations channel before a customer reports it.
- Website and phone menu publish one approved pricing lineup.
- Marketing reviews run on measured data: channel contribution from mix modeling and causal checks on campaign spend, on a recurring cadence.
- Arizona stays off the website and the Arizona Google profile and Oregon pages stay untouched until David says otherwise.

## Not in the end state

- No customer, staff, or admin dashboards in this repo; they live in AlphaFlux.
- No company reference that says only "pest control" where the company name belongs.
- No phone numbers, links, or email addresses inside Google Business Profile post text or artwork.
- No lead alert address guessed; Skyler's address is configured only when he or David confirms it.
